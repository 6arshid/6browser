Privacy policy text
6browser Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 16, 2026

6browser ("the app") is a web browser developed by 6arshid ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android and Windows.

1. Information we collect
We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting that sends data to us.

2. Information stored on your device
To work as a browser, the app stores the following only on your device:
• Browsing history, bookmarks, shortcuts and settings
• The list of your downloads
• Cookies, cache and website data saved by the web engine
You can delete this data at any time in the app (for example, History → Clear all) or by uninstalling the app. Private tabs do not save history. On Windows, their cookies and website data are kept in memory only and are removed when the app closes. On Android, the app clears cached website data when a private tab is closed.

3. Websites and search engines
When you visit a website or search, your request goes directly to that website or to the search engine you chose (Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing). These services may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not see or receive this traffic. To show site icons, the app loads each site's icon (favicon) from that site.

4. Importing from other browsers (Windows)
If you choose to import, the app reads bookmarks from other browsers installed on your computer and saves them in 6browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

5. Chrome themes
If you install a theme from the Chrome Web Store, the app downloads the theme file from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to that download.

6. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.

8. Contact
Questions about this policy: info@6arshid.com · www.6arshid.com

Submission checklist

Google Play

  • Create the app in Play Console (name, default language English (US), App, Free)
  • Build a signed App Bundle: flutter build appbundle --release with an upload key (Play needs an .aab, not an .apk)
  • Target the current required API level (check targetSdk in the Gradle build)
  • Main store listing: name, short and full description, icon, feature graphic, 2 to 8 phone screenshots
  • App content: privacy policy, ads (No), app access (all features available without login), content rating, target audience, data safety
  • New personal developer accounts: run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before production

Microsoft Store

  • Reserve the product name in Partner Center
  • Package as MSIX (signed by the Store) or submit the Inno Setup installer as an EXE app with an HTTPS URL
  • Properties: category, privacy policy URL, website, support contact, system requirements
  • Age ratings questionnaire (IARC)
  • Store listing (English): description, short description, what's new, features, search terms, screenshots, logos
  • Notes for certification (see above), including the runFullTrust justification
