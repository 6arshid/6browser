6browser Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

In short 6browser does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your history, bookmarks and settings stay on your device.

6browser ("the app") is a web browser for Android and Windows, developed by 6arshid ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account system and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting that sends data to us. We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

2. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Browsing history, bookmarks, New Tab shortcuts and settings
  • The list of your downloads (Windows)
  • Cookies, cache and website data saved by the web engine
  • Chrome themes you install

This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time in the app (for example, History → Clear all, or by removing bookmarks), or by uninstalling the app.

3. Private tabs

Private tabs do not save browsing history.

  • Windows: cookies and website data from private tabs are kept in memory only, are never written to disk, and are removed when the app closes.
  • Android: the app clears cached website data when a private tab is closed.

Private tabs do not hide your activity from the websites you visit, your employer, school or internet service provider.

4. Websites and search engines

When you visit a website or search, your request goes directly to that website or to the search engine you selected (Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing). These services may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not see or receive this traffic. To show site icons, the app loads each site's icon (favicon) from that site.

5. Importing from other browsers (Windows)

If you choose to import, the app reads bookmarks from other browsers installed on your computer (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave and Mozilla Firefox) or from a bookmarks file you select, and saves them in 6browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

6. Chrome themes

If you install a theme from the Chrome Web Store, the app downloads the theme file from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to that download.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access: needed to load web pages.
  • File access: only when you choose to save a download, import bookmarks or load a theme file.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your operating system and the app up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and publish the new version at this address.
